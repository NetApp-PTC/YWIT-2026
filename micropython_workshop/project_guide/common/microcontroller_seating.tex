\subsubsection{Attach the microcontroller to the breadboard}
Carefully insert the pins at the bottom of your microcontroller into the breadboard, making sure that the microcontroller is oriented such that:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The pin labeled \textbf{5V} is inserted in hole at \textbf{Column H, Row 1} of the breadboard (or \bbhole{mcu.5v}{H1}, for short)
    \item The pin labeled \textbf{GPIO2} is inserted in hole \bbhole{mcu.gpio2}{D1} of the breadboard
    \item The pin labeled \textbf{GPIO20} is inserted in hole \bbhole{mcu.gpio20}{H7} of the breadboard
    \item the pin labeled \textbf{GPIO21} is inserted in hole \bbhole{mcu.gpio21}{D7} of the breadboard
\end{itemize}
You may need to apply more pressure than expected to seat the microcontroller properly in the breadboard. When its over, it should look like this:
