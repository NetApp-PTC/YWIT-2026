\begin{tcolorbox}[colback=yellow!10!white,colframe=yellow!50!black]
    \textbf{Which resistor is which?} Both resistors are small and blue, with five colored bands and
    no printing. Just count the \textbf{red} bands: the \textbf{220\si{\ohm}} resistor has \textbf{two
    red bands next to each other}, and the \textbf{10\si{\kilo\ohm}} resistor has \textbf{exactly
    one}. Swapping them won't damage anything, but the circuit won't behave as described, so ask an
    instructor if unsure.
